Mission To enable institutions and communities to reduce the Recognition–Action Gap through research, education, and the advancement of governance frameworks and legal tools; developing, testing, and disseminating practical methodologies for high-consequence systems.

Principles, Values & Beliefs CORE VALUES

Scientific Rigor
We commit to precision in observation, transparent methodologies, and evidence that withstands independent scrutiny. We strive to document our research, encourage external review whenever appropriate, and promote transparency consistent with ethical, legal, and partnership obligations.

Independence
We maintain institutional autonomy to speak truth about governance failures and systemic risk, without political or financial constraint. We report findings regardless of stakeholder preferences.

Accountability
We document failure. We publish cases. We maintain auditable systems for all programs and findings. We hold ourselves and our partners responsible for both action and inaction.

Prevention Before Reaction
We invest in anticipatory capacity and early intervention rather than accepting disaster as inevitable. We pilot methodologies before scaling.

Equity & Co-Responsibility
Those who benefit from a system bear responsibility for its sustainability. Governance frameworks must distribute both burden and benefit fairly.

Selective Collaboration
We partner with credible institutions committed to measurable institutional change, transparent reporting, and long-term accountability.
